Small enough
to forget.

38 mm across, 9.8 mm thick, and a dial that reads at a glance. The case is brushed on the flanks and polished on the bezel so the light catches only where you want it to.

The movement is visible through the back. Four wheels, three bridges, one balance, all of it doing something you can watch.

Two finishes,
one object.

01

Cooler and more precise. The same watch with the temperature turned down.

Turn it over and the movement is the dial. Bridges, wheels and a blued balance spring.
