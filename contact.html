Usual

Is the watch for sale?+

No. MERIDIAN is a fictional brand and the study exists as a design demonstration only.

How often does it need servicing?+

Every five years, or whenever it starts losing more than ten seconds a day.

Can I change the strap?+

Yes. Quick-release bars, 20 mm, and the tool is in the box.

Brass or steel?+

Brass if you want the watch to age with you. Steel if you want it to look the same in ten years.
