Atelier notes

Why still
hand-wound.

An automatic watch winds itself and asks nothing of you. We think that is the wrong trade.

The daily
ten seconds.

Winding is a small, deliberate act. Thirty turns in the morning, the resistance building under your fingers, and the watch is ready for another day.

It also means the watch needs you. Stop winding and it stops. That relationship is the whole idea.

Cut and shape

Bridges are cut, then bevelled by hand under a loupe.

Finish

Côtes de Genève on the bridges, circular graining on the wheels.

Assemble

Wheels, spring, balance, and a movement that runs before it is cased.

Regulate

Six positions, then a week on the bench before it ships.

Made to be
opened.

The caseback is held by six screws, not pressed on. Any competent watchmaker can open it, and the movement is designed so that it can be taken apart and put back together.

A watch that cannot be repaired is a disposable object. This one is meant to outlast the person who winds it first.
