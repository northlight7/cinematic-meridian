THE ART OF PAYING ATTENTION

A small world.
In motion.

A hand-wound watch makes time tangible. A turn of the crown becomes stored energy. Gears carry it forward. A balance gives it rhythm.

01 / STORE

The spring

Winding gathers energy into a coiled spring. The ritual begins with your hand.

02 / TRANSMIT

The train

A connected set of wheels carries that energy from the spring toward the hands.

03 / REGULATE

The heartbeat

The balance oscillates, helping an escapement release energy in measured steps.

The interactive movement is an artistic study of these principles, not a technical model of a production calibre.
